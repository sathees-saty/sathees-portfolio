My journey alongside the S/4HANA release milestones

2015SAP S/4HANA 1511 • November 2015

Building my enterprise ERP foundation

This milestone represents the beginning of the S/4HANA era. My delivery lens at this stage is rooted in understanding how ERP supports end-to-end business operations and how project teams translate business requirements into a controlled implementation plan.

  • Understand core enterprise processes across finance, procurement, sales, inventory and operations.
  • Establish scope, stakeholder ownership, requirements traceability and delivery governance.
  • Connect business process decisions with data, reporting and downstream integrations.
My leadership focus: Establish a shared business view of scope, outcomes, ownership and delivery milestones.
2016SAP S/4HANA 1610 • October 2016

Connecting business processes across functions

As the S/4HANA journey matured, I focus on the cross-functional nature of ERP delivery. A change in one process can affect finance, supply chain, sales, reporting and the customer experience.

  • Drive workshops that bring business owners and functional teams to common decisions.
  • Manage dependencies across process areas and external applications.
  • Track decisions, gaps, risks and scope changes before they affect the critical path.
My leadership focus: Manage the programme as one connected business transformation, not as isolated module implementations.
2017SAP S/4HANA 1709 • September 2017

Strengthening implementation planning and controls

My project-management approach emphasises disciplined planning: a realistic integrated schedule, clear workstream ownership, resource capacity, decision governance and measurable readiness criteria.

  • Build work breakdown structures, integrated plans, RAID logs and RACI matrices.
  • Coordinate functional design, technical development, integration and testing plans.
  • Use stage gates to make progress, risks and unresolved decisions visible.
My leadership focus: Convert a complex implementation scope into an executable plan with accountable owners.
2018SAP S/4HANA 1809 • September 2018

Driving process standardisation and fit-to-standard thinking

I place greater emphasis on business-led process decisions. The objective is to understand where standard processes meet business needs, where genuine differentiation is required, and what the cost of additional complexity will be.

  • Facilitate fit-to-standard discussions and document justified process gaps.
  • Separate mandatory business requirements from preferences and legacy habits.
  • Assess customisation impact on testing, upgrades, support and long-term ownership.
My leadership focus: Protect business value while avoiding unnecessary complexity and uncontrolled customisation.
2019SAP S/4HANA 1909 • September 2019

Managing data, integration and end-to-end quality

Enterprise transformation depends on more than configuring business processes. Data quality, interfaces, reconciliation, security and end-to-end testing determine whether the solution works across the organisation.

  • Coordinate data profiling, cleansing, mapping, migration rehearsals and reconciliation.
  • Manage interface dependencies between SAP and third-party platforms.
  • Plan SIT, UAT, regression, defect triage and business sign-off around real scenarios.
My leadership focus: Treat data and integration as programme-critical workstreams with clear entry and exit criteria.
2020SAP S/4HANA 2020 • October 2020

Leading delivery through complex operating conditions

My programme-management perspective gives strong attention to distributed delivery, transparent reporting and continuity. Teams need a common view of priorities, decisions, risks and dependencies even when delivery is spread across locations and partners.

  • Run predictable governance across business, functional, technical, testing and vendor teams.
  • Use milestone-based reporting and evidence-based status rather than activity-only updates.
  • Keep scope, schedule, budget, quality and resource risks connected in one integrated view.
My leadership focus: Maintain delivery control and stakeholder confidence across distributed teams.
2021SAP S/4HANA 2021 • October 2021

Expanding the transformation view to cloud and adoption

Cloud transformation makes operating model, subscription choices, service responsibilities, security, integration and adoption important programme decisions. I approach these as business and delivery topics, not infrastructure-only decisions.

  • Assess cloud deployment considerations, environments, connectivity and operational responsibilities.
  • Coordinate change impact assessment, role-based training and business readiness.
  • Prepare support transition, hypercare ownership and service acceptance criteria.
My leadership focus: Plan for the organisation’s ability to operate and adopt the solution, not only to go live.
2022SAP S/4HANA 2022 • October 2022

Leading broader, multi-workstream transformation

My progression into senior project and programme leadership brings a portfolio-level view: aligning business outcomes, multiple workstreams, delivery partners, release priorities and executive decisions.

  • Integrate business process, application, data, infrastructure, security and change plans.
  • Manage cross-programme dependencies, critical decisions, risks and financial controls.
  • Use steering committees and workstream reviews to resolve blockers at the right level.
My leadership focus: Align multiple teams around one transformation roadmap and a consistent definition of success.
2023SAP S/4HANA 2023 • October 2023

Emphasising clean core, extensibility and integration

Modern SAP programmes need an intentional approach to extensions and integrations. I focus on protecting the upgrade path while enabling the business capabilities that create differentiation.

  • Promote clean-core principles and challenge unnecessary modifications.
  • Evaluate extension and integration patterns, API ownership and lifecycle support.
  • Coordinate architecture decisions with delivery cost, operational impact and business value.
My leadership focus: Make architecture choices visible as business trade-offs, delivery risks and long-term operating costs.
2024SAP S/4HANA Cloud • 2024 delivery landscape

Bringing together cloud ERP, platform and intelligent capabilities

My current capability narrative brings together S/4HANA transformation, SAP BTP, SAP Integration Suite, analytics, automation and the wider enterprise application landscape. The emphasis is on selecting capabilities that solve an evidenced business problem.

  • Connect ERP roadmaps with integration, analytics, workflow and application-extension needs.
  • Evaluate business cases, delivery dependencies, data readiness and adoption impacts.
  • Manage proof-of-concept outcomes separately from production implementation claims.
My leadership focus: Turn technology options into prioritised business use cases, governed roadmaps and measurable outcomes.
2025SAP S/4HANA 2025 • October 8, 2025

Connecting ERP transformation with AI-enabled business outcomes

At this milestone, I position my SAP journey around business-led transformation and responsible use of AI. My work includes leading a large-scale SAP AI invoice-automation proof of concept that resulted in a working demo; it is represented as a PoC, not as a production rollout or a claim of measured savings.

  • Identify practical AI opportunities in finance and enterprise workflows, such as invoice handling and exception support.
  • Define business ownership, data quality, human review, security and governance requirements.
  • Use a value-led roadmap with clear PoC success criteria, production-readiness gates and benefit measurement.
My leadership focus: Bridge ERP process knowledge, programme governance and AI opportunities while keeping outcomes, controls and delivery evidence at the centre.
