GROW with SAP and RISE with SAP

Technical Capabilities • SAP S/4HANA Cloud Public Edition • SAP Cloud ERP Private

GROW with SAP and RISE with SAP support different ERP adoption and transformation paths. At a high level, GROW aligns with adopting SAP S/4HANA Cloud Public Edition through standardized, fit-to-standard processes. RISE aligns with a broader business transformation journey centered on SAP Cloud ERP Private. Exact scope, services and commercial terms depend on the current SAP offering and customer contract.

Architecture principle: Select the ERP edition and operating model that best fit business processes, customization needs, integration complexity, compliance requirements and the organization’s ability to adopt standard processes.

1. Definitions

GROW with SAP

GROW with SAP is SAP’s offering for organizations adopting SAP S/4HANA Cloud Public Edition, especially those seeking a standardized cloud ERP foundation, guided adoption and faster value realization through fit-to-standard processes.

  • ERP foundation: SAP S/4HANA Cloud Public Edition.
  • Approach: Fit-to-standard, SAP Activate guidance and predefined scope where suitable.
  • Extensibility: Prefer configuration and governed side-by-side extensions using SAP BTP where appropriate.
  • Operations: SAP manages the underlying cloud service; customers still manage business configuration, roles, data, integrations, testing and adoption.

RISE with SAP

RISE with SAP is a business-transformation offering centered on SAP Cloud ERP Private, commonly SAP S/4HANA Cloud Private Edition. It can suit organizations with complex existing SAP landscapes, established custom processes or a need for greater ERP flexibility. Confirm the contracted technical services and responsibility split before planning.

  • ERP foundation: SAP Cloud ERP Private / SAP S/4HANA Cloud Private Edition.
  • Approach: Supports ERP transition and modernization, including eligible existing-landscape transformation paths.
  • Extensibility: Offers broader ERP flexibility, while clean-core and upgradeability remain important goals.
  • Operations: Cloud and managed-service responsibilities depend on the contract; customers retain responsibility for business processes, data, access, integrations and governance.

3. When to Choose GROW with SAP

  • The organization is implementing a new ERP or replacing disconnected systems.
  • Business leaders are willing to adopt standard SAP processes and avoid unnecessary customization.
  • Requirements align with SAP S/4HANA Cloud Public Edition and its supported extension model.
  • Repeatable implementation, regular cloud innovation and faster value realization are priorities.
  • Process owners can align master data and integrations to a fit-to-standard approach.

Illustrative project: A growing distributor replaces spreadsheets and disconnected finance, sales and purchasing applications. It adopts standard order-to-cash, procure-to-pay and record-to-report processes, integrating e-commerce, warehouse and banking services through supported interfaces.

4. When to Choose RISE with SAP

  • The organization has an established SAP ECC or SAP S/4HANA landscape with significant process and integration dependencies.
  • Business-critical processes require a private-cloud ERP environment or capabilities aligned to SAP Cloud ERP Private.
  • The program includes transition from legacy ERP, custom code, interfaces and existing operational practices.
  • It needs to modernize the ERP landscape while preserving justified differentiating processes.
  • Migration, custom-code remediation, data conversion and coexistence need substantial planning.

Illustrative project: A multinational manufacturer runs SAP ECC with custom production planning, plant systems and regional interfaces. It plans a phased move to SAP Cloud ERP Private, including custom-code assessment, data migration and interface modernization.

7. Real-World Project Examples

The following are illustrative industry scenarios, not claims about named customer deployments.

Example A — GROW with SAP: Mid-Market Distribution

  • Challenge: Disconnected accounting, sales orders, purchasing and inventory records.
  • Approach: Adopt SAP S/4HANA Cloud Public Edition using fit-to-standard workshops and controlled scope.
  • Integration: Connect e-commerce, warehouse, tax/e-invoicing and banking interfaces using supported APIs or integration services.
  • Delivery focus: Master-data quality, process-owner decisions, standard configuration, UAT and adoption.
  • Measures: Process adoption, order/invoice cycle time, data accuracy, interface reliability and financial close performance.

Example B — RISE with SAP: Global Manufacturing

  • Challenge: Legacy SAP ECC, custom code, plant systems and regional interfaces create high support and change costs.
  • Approach: Assess the landscape, define a SAP Cloud ERP Private target and plan phased migration.
  • Integration: Modernize interfaces with MES, WMS, procurement, planning, HR and analytics platforms.
  • Delivery focus: Custom-code assessment, data conversion, process harmonization, cutover rehearsals and regional readiness.
  • Measures: Migration reconciliation, process continuity, interface stability, adoption and technical-debt reduction.

8. Project Manager's Responsibilities

Strategy & Selection

  • Align ERP strategy with business outcomes, operating model and transformation appetite.
  • Coordinate fit-gap assessment and validate public versus private edition requirements with SAP solution architects.
  • Assess process standardization, customization, integration, compliance and lifecycle needs—not only cost.

Planning & Governance

  • Define scope, workstreams, roadmap, milestones, phase gates, budget and resource plan.
  • Establish steering committee, design authority, decision log, RAID process and change control.
  • Coordinate SAP, implementation partner, business, data, integration, security and infrastructure teams.
  • Document the responsibility matrix, including contracted cloud-service boundaries.

Delivery & Quality

  • Drive fit-to-standard workshops, configuration decisions and design sign-offs.
  • Track data migration, integration, security, testing, performance and non-functional requirements.
  • Manage custom-code remediation and dependencies for private-cloud transformation where applicable.
  • Manage public-cloud release readiness and regression testing for GROW/public-cloud programs.
  • Ensure UAT covers end-to-end processes, exceptions, controls and reconciliations.

Cutover, Adoption & Operations

  • Coordinate mock cutovers, deployment sequencing, rollback planning and go/no-go criteria.
  • Drive role-based training, business readiness, communications and adoption tracking.
  • Confirm monitoring, support model, service levels, runbooks and escalation paths.
  • Measure benefits realization and stabilize operations through hypercare.

9. Key Risks and Controls

  • Incorrect edition selection: Validate critical requirements through fit-gap and architecture assessment.
  • Excessive customization: Use design authority and clean-core principles; justify exceptions by business value and lifecycle impact.
  • Data quality: Assign data owners, profile source data early and run migration rehearsals.
  • Integration gaps: Maintain an interface inventory and test end-to-end flows with source and target owners.
  • Responsibility ambiguity: Document contracted ownership for security, operations, backup, recovery and service management.
  • Change resistance: Engage process owners early and measure role-based readiness and adoption.

10. Key Program Metrics

  • Fit-to-standard decision closure and scope stability
  • Data migration reconciliation and defect rate
  • Integration test pass rate and interface reliability
  • Critical business-process test coverage
  • Custom-code remediation progress where applicable
  • Schedule, budget, risks and milestone predictability
  • User training completion and adoption
  • Business KPIs such as order cycle time, inventory accuracy and financial close duration
  • Production incidents, resolution time and service-level compliance

Key Takeaway

GROW with SAP and RISE with SAP are not simply two names for the same cloud ERP deployment. GROW generally aligns with standardized adoption of SAP S/4HANA Cloud Public Edition, while RISE generally aligns with a broader transformation journey centered on SAP Cloud ERP Private. Select the path after validating business-process fit, customization, landscape complexity, integration needs, operating responsibilities and the current SAP commercial offering.
