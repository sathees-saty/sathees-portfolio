Case Study 01

SAP S/4HANA Transformation

🌐 Domain: SAP / ERP Transformation
💼 Role: Senior Program / Project Manager

The Challenge

The organization was operating on a legacy SAP ERP landscape with complex business processes, integration dependencies and modernization requirements. The transformation required a structured roadmap for moving toward SAP S/4HANA while minimizing business disruption.

Approach & Solution

  • Led program planning and transformation roadmap covering business, technology, data and integration workstreams.
  • Coordinated SAP S/4HANA Greenfield / Brownfield implementation activities across multiple teams.
  • Established program governance covering scope, schedule, risks, dependencies, financials and executive reporting.
  • Facilitated fit-to-standard workshops and alignment between business stakeholders and technical teams.
  • Coordinated integration, testing, migration, cutover and Go-Live readiness activities.
  • Managed stakeholder communication across business, SAP, implementation partners and technology teams.
Program Impact
Established a structured transformation framework for SAP modernization and business readiness.
Program Management Takeaway: Successful ERP transformation requires alignment of business strategy, technology architecture, data, people and delivery governance.
