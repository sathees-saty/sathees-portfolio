Case Study 02

PACS Migration Program

🌐 Domain: Healthcare / Medical Imaging
🏥 Scope: Radiology • Cardiology • Hematology
💼 Role: Program / Project Manager
🔄 Transformation: GE PACS → Change PACS

The Challenge

The healthcare organization required migration from the existing GE PACS platform to the Change PACS environment while maintaining continuity of clinical imaging services. The program covered multiple clinical areas including Radiology, Cardiology and Hematology and required coordination across clinical users, application teams, infrastructure teams, imaging specialists, vendors and healthcare stakeholders.

Program Objectives

  • Migrate medical imaging data and workflows from GE PACS to Change PACS.
  • Maintain continuity of clinical imaging services throughout the migration.
  • Support Radiology, Cardiology and Hematology imaging workflows.
  • Validate DICOM connectivity and interoperability with downstream and upstream systems.
  • Coordinate clinical validation, user acceptance and operational readiness.
  • Execute phased Go-Live with controlled risk and defined rollback procedures.

01 — Initiation

Established the program foundation, governance structure, objectives, scope and stakeholder alignment required to initiate the PACS transformation.

  • Defined program vision, objectives, scope and success criteria.
  • Identified executive sponsors, clinical stakeholders, application owners, infrastructure teams and implementation partners.
  • Established program governance, communication cadence and escalation mechanisms.
  • Defined initial RAID log covering risks, assumptions, issues and dependencies.
  • Established high-level milestones and program delivery roadmap.

02 — Discovery & Assessment

Performed detailed discovery of the existing GE PACS environment, clinical workflows, integrations, interfaces and data landscape.

  • Assessed the existing GE PACS architecture, applications, storage and integration landscape.
  • Documented Radiology, Cardiology and Hematology workflows and clinical dependencies.
  • Identified DICOM modalities, imaging sources, consumers and integration endpoints.
  • Assessed integration with EHR, RIS and other clinical systems.
  • Identified historical imaging data volumes, retention requirements and migration priorities.
  • Conducted dependency and risk assessment for clinical operations.

03 — Planning

Developed a detailed migration strategy and execution plan designed to minimize clinical disruption and provide controlled transition to Change PACS.

  • Developed the end-to-end PACS migration roadmap.
  • Defined migration waves based on clinical department, modality, data volume and operational dependencies.
  • Created detailed Work Breakdown Structure (WBS), milestones and delivery plans.
  • Defined data migration, validation and reconciliation strategy.
  • Developed test strategy covering integration, workflow, performance and user acceptance testing.
  • Established cutover, rollback and business continuity plans.
  • Defined Go-Live readiness criteria and clinical sign-off process.

04 — Execution

Coordinated cross-functional execution across healthcare stakeholders, technical teams, vendors and clinical users.

  • Managed workstreams across application, infrastructure, networking, imaging and clinical operations teams.
  • Coordinated DICOM configuration and interface validation.
  • Managed system integration and workflow validation.
  • Coordinated data migration preparation and validation activities.
  • Conducted regular program governance meetings, status reporting and risk reviews.
  • Managed vendor dependencies, technical issues and escalation resolution.
  • Coordinated user training and operational readiness activities.

05 — Migration

Executed controlled migration of imaging data and associated workflows from GE PACS to Change PACS using a phased and validated approach.

  • Prepared source and target PACS environments for migration.
  • Executed migration waves based on agreed clinical priorities.
  • Performed data integrity and reconciliation checks.
  • Validated patient, study and imaging metadata.
  • Coordinated clinical validation of migrated studies.
  • Monitored migration progress, exceptions and remediation activities.
  • Maintained detailed migration tracking and executive reporting.

06 — Go-Live in Phases

Implemented a phased Go-Live approach to reduce operational risk and allow controlled validation before expanding the migration to additional clinical areas.

  • Established Go-Live command center and hypercare support structure.
  • Executed initial Go-Live wave with controlled clinical validation.
  • Monitored system performance, DICOM workflows, integrations and user adoption.
  • Addressed issues identified during the initial Go-Live before subsequent migration waves.
  • Progressively transitioned additional Radiology, Cardiology and Hematology workflows.
  • Conducted Go-Live readiness reviews and stakeholder sign-offs for each phase.
  • Maintained rollback and contingency procedures throughout the phased deployment.

07 — Hypercare

Established focused post-Go-Live support to stabilize clinical workflows and ensure operational continuity.

  • Established daily operational and clinical support reviews.
  • Monitored incidents, defects and workflow issues.
  • Prioritized and coordinated rapid issue resolution.
  • Monitored PACS availability, integrations and clinical workflow performance.
  • Supported clinical users during transition from GE PACS to Change PACS.
  • Transitioned stabilized operations to the BAU support organization.

08 — Program Closure

Completed formal program closure after successful migration, stabilization and transition to business-as-usual operations.

  • Confirmed completion of migration scope and clinical acceptance.
  • Completed outstanding issue and defect closure.
  • Obtained stakeholder and business sign-offs.
  • Completed knowledge transfer and operational handover.
  • Finalized program documentation and lessons learned.
  • Transitioned the solution into steady-state operational support.

Clinical Areas Covered

Radiology Diagnostic imaging workflows, modalities, DICOM integration and clinical validation.
Cardiology Cardiac imaging workflows, integration and clinical validation.
Hematology Department workflows, imaging/data integration and user validation.

Technology & Integration Landscape

  • GE PACS — Legacy Source Platform
  • Change PACS — Target Platform
  • DICOM — Medical Imaging Communication
  • RIS — Radiology Information System
  • EHR / Clinical Systems Integration
  • Medical Imaging Modalities
  • Healthcare Integration Interfaces
Program Impact
Delivered a structured, phased PACS transformation approach covering Radiology, Cardiology and Hematology while maintaining clinical workflow continuity and providing controlled transition from GE PACS to Change PACS.
Program Management Takeaway: Healthcare technology transformation requires disciplined planning, clinical stakeholder engagement, integration governance, phased migration, strong Go-Live controls and focused hypercare to protect patient-care operations.
